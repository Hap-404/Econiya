ANDROID IS MOBILE

EC CD765

The EC CD765 featured IS phone is designed for maximum safety in the field and enhanced usability for first responders. It is engineered to operate safely in hazardous environments, keeping people safe and connected while they get their jobs done.

This phone is especially designed to work in a potentially explosive area and the waterproof feature, with very light housing and nice appearance adds to the overall usability of the model. The battery can last for more than 10hrs making it easy to use for any rugged environment. It's a powerful mobile phone for zone 1/21 with Android operating system. The rugged key-operated phone provides many technical advantages: WiFi, Android, LED-torch, amplified loudspeaker and SOS-key.
