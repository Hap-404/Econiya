WHO WE ARE

Powering Industrial Innovation Worldwide

FourBahaus Solutions is a leading designer, manufacturer, and system integrator of high-performance communication and industrial automation solutions. We specialize in Ex/IS-certified wireless telephony, radio communication, water network automation, photometric water analysis, instrumentation, and explosion-proof automation for fire and gas industries. Our solutions are engineered to deliver reliability, safety, and operational excellence across critical industrial environments.

As a trusted technology partner, we have built robust communication networks for industries and police departments across India. We also support water utilities, Indian Railways, and municipal corporations with innovative automation solutions, helping organizations improve efficiency, strengthen critical infrastructure, enhance workplace safety, and achieve sustainable, long-term operational success through advanced engineering and dependable technologies.
