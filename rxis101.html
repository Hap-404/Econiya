ANDROID IS MOBILE

RxIS101

The Realix RxIS101 is an advanced device specifically designed for professionals working in hazardous environments. It offers a perfect blend of exceptional safety features and cutting-edge technology.

With its 4G connectivity and dual SIM capability, staying connected with your team has never been easier. The RxIS101 operates on the latest Android 12 operating system, providing a seamless and secure user experience. Its compact 2.83-inch TFT screen ensures clear visibility in any lighting conditions.

Thanks to its long-lasting 4200 mAh battery, the RxIS101 keeps you productive throughout your workday. Real-time communication is made effortless with its Push-to-Talk over cellular functionality.

Built to endure tough conditions, the RxIS101 holds an IP68 rating, making it dust-proof, waterproof, and resistant to shocks and vibrations.

Additionally, it features NFC technology for secure data transfer and contactless transactions. With its powerful 120 dB sound output, you can communicate clearly even in noisy environments.

In case of emergencies, the easily accessible SOS button can be used to signal for assistance. The RxIS101 also comes equipped with a 16MP rear camera, allowing you to capture important visuals with precision.

Choose the Realix RxIS101 and unlock the ultimate combination of safety, performance, and innovation in an intrinsically safe mobile device.
